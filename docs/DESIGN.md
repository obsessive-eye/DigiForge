## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the balance between high-end digital craftsmanship and sovereign content protection. Built for photographers, digital artists, independent creators, and media students, the interface bridges pro-grade creative software and accessible cryptographic security without lapsing into stereotypical "cybersecurity" tropes.

### Visual Philosophy
- **Restrained Precision:** An Apple-caliber design language grounded in dark studio elegance. Surfaces feel tailored, intentional, and quiet, ensuring creative assets remain the primary focal point.
- **Human-Centric Security:** Complex cryptographic processes (provenance stamping, watermarking, perceptual hashing, key derivation) are presented through progressive disclosure, calm microcopy, and clear status feedback rather than dense technical terminal screens.
- **Atmospheric Contrast:** Monochromatic obsidian and deep navy foundations accented by a singular, luminous cyan tone reserved for focus, critical affordances, and active integrity states.

### Emotional Resonance
Interactions feel weightless, reassuring, and unhurried. Users experience confidence, artistic dignity, and unquestioned control over their intellectual property.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authentic, deep dark-mode baseline that eliminates pure black in favor of deep oceanic midnight tones. This prevents visual harshness on OLED displays while maximizing photographic contrast.

### Palette Roles
- **Base Canvas (`#080D18`):** The master canvas tone. Deep, low-fatigue slate navy providing high dynamic range for imagery.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#101827`):** Recessed containers, tool shelves, canvas backdrops, and navigation rails.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#151F30`):** Elevated cards, context menus, inspector panels, and modal shells.
- **Surface Outline / Border (`#283548`):** Subtle 1px boundary layer used to define edges against stacked surfaces.
- **Primary Vibrant Cyan (`#20E6D2`):** Reserved for primary interactive elements, active selection rings, verified watermarking badges, and processing anchors. Used selectively to avoid aesthetic exhaustion.
- **Text Primary (`#F4F7FB`):** High-clarity off-white for primary headlines, active values, and primary interface controls.
- **Text Secondary (`#A7B2C3`):** Neutral slate tone for labels, descriptive copy, metadata, and inactive icons.
- **Success Emerald (`#39D9A5`):** Cryptographic verification, intact signature validations, and successful sync confirmations.
- **Subdued Alert Red (`#FF727F`):** Tamper alerts, signature mismatches, and destructive action triggers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances expressive structure with effortless legibility:

- **Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Provides structured geometric rhythm with human proportions. Generous negative tracking at large scales prevents loose glyph spacing while preserving distinctiveness.
- **Body & Controls (Hanken Grotesk):** Chosen for its tall x-height, neutral posture, and supreme clarity across dark surfaces. Used for all narrative copy, field values, and user-facing dialogs.
- **Cryptographic & Technical Strings (Space Mono):** Strictly isolated to non-narrative technical markers: SHA-256 digests, signature tokens, EXIF coordinate readouts, and cryptographic keys. Never used for general body text or standard navigation labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a proportional, responsive fluid grid optimized for dual-mode workflows: creation workspace mode (dockable inspector, centered canvas) and catalog gallery mode.

### Grid Foundations
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a flexible outer margin pegged at minimum 40px (`2.5rem`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px margins. Inspector panels collapse into floating slide-overs.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) safe-area margins. Bottom sheets replace modals and side drawers.

### Spacing Cadence
Spacing follows an 8-point base progression, with 4px intervals for internal component padding. Generous vertical breathing room is enforced between distinct workflow zones to reduce cognitive overhead when auditing critical copyright parameters.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through subtle tonal step-ups, calibrated 1px perimeter definition, and gentle light dissipation rather than stark drop shadows.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Layer (`#080D18`):** Absolute bottom. Raw canvas backplates, background views, and photo preview voids.
2. **Structural Layer (`#101827`):** Side navigation rails, toolboxes, and segmented control containers. Borders: `1px solid #283548`.
3. **Elevated Card & Inspector Layer (`#151F30`):** Asset cards, detail inspectors, filter panels. Border: `1px solid rgba(40, 53, 72, 0.75)`. Shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
4. **Floating Overlays & Modals:** Key derivation sheets, signature verification panels, context menus. Surface color: `rgba(21, 31, 48, 0.85)` with a backdrop blur of `20px`. Border: `1px solid rgba(32, 230, 210, 0.2)`. Shadow: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.

### Lighting & Ambient Highlights
To emulate Apple-style glass and physical dark anodized aluminum, primary elevated containers feature an ultra-subtle top-edge inner highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(244, 247, 251, 0.06)`).

## Shapes

The design system uses smooth squircle-like radii to convey polish and approachability. 

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Chips:** Set to `0.5rem` (8px) for precise, functional ergonomics.
- **Cards and Panels (`rounded-lg`):** Scaled to `1rem` (16px) to comfortably wrap dense metadata and thumbnail grids.
- **Modals, Floating Toolbars, and Main Dialogs (`rounded-xl`):** Scaled to `1.5rem` (24px) for a soft, human-friendly framing.
- **Avatars, Status Dots, and Floating Toggles:** Rendered as full pills or circles (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#20E6D2`, label `#080D18` (Space Grotesk Medium), border: none. On hover, subtle luminance elevation without scale change. Active state dims opacity to `0.9`. Focus ring: `2px solid #20E6D2` offset by 2px in `#080D18`.
- **Secondary:** Background `#151F30`, label `#F4F7FB`, border: `1px solid #283548`. On hover, background shifts to `#1C2A40` with border tinting toward `#20E6D2` at 30% opacity.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, label `#A7B2C3`. On hover, label transitions to `#F4F7FB` over a subtle background tint (`rgba(244, 247, 251, 0.04)`).
- **Destructive:** Background `rgba(255, 114, 127, 0.12)`, label `#FF727F`, border: `1px solid rgba(255, 114, 127, 0.3)`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Text Inputs:** Background `#101827`, border `1px solid #283548`, text `#F4F7FB`, placeholder `#A7B2C3` at 60% opacity. Rounded: `8px`. Padding: `10px 14px`. On focus: border becomes `#20E6D2` with zero outer neon glow; only a crisp 1px clean ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Unchecked: 18px box/circle, background `#101827`, border `1.5px solid #283548`. Checked: background `#20E6D2`, border `#20E6D2`, icon/dot `#080D18`.

### Chips & Metadata Tags
- **Status Chips:** Low-saturation backgrounds with high-contrast text. Verified: background `rgba(57, 217, 165, 0.12)`, text `#39D9A5`, border `1px solid rgba(57, 217, 165, 0.25)`.
- **Hash / Key Pills:** Background `#101827`, border `1px solid #283548`, typography `Space Mono` (`mono-badge`), text `#A7B2C3`. Includes a one-click copy affordance.

### Cards & Asset Tiles
- Structural container with `#151F30` fill, `16px` border-radius, and `1px solid #283548` outline.
- Media viewport maintains a 16:10 or 1:1 aspect ratio with dark placeholder shading.
- Bottom metadata shelf houses title, date, protection badge (e.g., "Signed C2PA"), and subtle overflow action menu.

### Specialized Protection Components
- **Provenance Inspector Panel:** A collapsible side drawer exhibiting cryptographic chain-of-custody. Shows visual node paths connected by thin 1px `#283548` lines, author identity cards, and tamper detection indicators with soft emerald/red status icons.
- **Perceptual Watermark Slider:** Dual-handle control with real-time preview masking; active track filled with `#20E6D2`, thumb featuring high-contrast off-white core.
